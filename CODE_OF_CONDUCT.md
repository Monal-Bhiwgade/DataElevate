# Code of Conduct

DataElevate follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give and accept constructive
feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the project and its users. Harassment,
insults, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email the maintainer at
**3051monal@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately and as quickly as possible.
The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who break this
code of conduct.
